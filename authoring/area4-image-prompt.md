## Concept request

Create a high-resolution low-poly painterly 3D world map, matching Layout.png's
established miniature aesthetic. North is up. Area 2 spans the full north; Area 1's
green warm-roof village and lake sit middle-left; Area 3's walled ruined citadel sits
middle-right. Preserve all three existing connections.

Extend south with a wide Area 4 spanning both Areas 1 and 3. One continuous dark
east-west rift separates their south edges from Area 4's north edge. Exactly two
north-south bridges cross it: forged skeletal iron from Area 1, and a barely hanging,
partly ruined timber/rope bridge from Area 3's new south gate. Both retain a walkable
central deck. Remove the original image's obsolete west-gate-to-Area-4 label.

Area 4 has charcoal ash ground, scorched trunks, clear paths, several small isolated
orange lava pools, and exactly one ruined stone throne in its southern half, about
three times human height, with a human scale reference. No volcano, lava river,
additional castle, extra bridge, or extra region. Keep every area visible.

Labels: “Area 1”, “Area 2”, “Area 3”, “Area 4”, “Burned Forest”, “Rift / Abyss”,
“Forged skeletal bridge”, “Ruined timber bridge”,
“Ancient stone throne · 3× human scale”. Include a small north arrow.

## Resolution refinement prompt (verbatim)

Increase the resolution of this approved world-layout concept to a high-resolution
PNG, 3072 × 2560 pixels. Preserve the entire composition, all four areas and every
label exactly. Keep the two southern bridges, continuous dark rift, small isolated
lava pools and single ruined throne unchanged in design and position. Reconstruct
fine rock, tree, bridge and masonry detail cleanly at the larger resolution; avoid
blur and sharpening halos. Do not crop, add objects, move paths, rewrite labels, or
change the painterly low-poly 3D aesthetic. The output must be the complete map at
3072 × 2560 pixels or greater, not a small preview.
